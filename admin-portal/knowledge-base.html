For bilateral lower back or sciatica pain:

  1. Channel 1 Electrode Placement: Position horizontally on lumbar spine level L4-S1 or flanking the spine by 1.5 inches.
  2. Channel 2 Electrode Placement: Align along the dermatomal radiating pain pathway (upper gluteal / posterior thigh).
  3. Recommended Protocol Settings: Continuous Mode, Pulse Rate 80–100 Hz, Pulse Width 150 μs. Increase intensity dial slowly until tingling is robust and comforting without muscle fasciculation.
